# π0.5 + Real-Time Chunking

Parseable spec: [`../notes/06_option_specs.md`](../notes/06_option_specs.md#8-π05--real-time-chunking).

- **Network:** [lerobot/pi05_base](https://huggingface.co/lerobot/pi05_base) (~3.6B). LIBERO: [lerobot/pi05_libero](https://huggingface.co/lerobot/pi05_libero). No separate S1; chunks + RTC.
- **Finetune just to start?** **No.** `lerobot-eval` + [pi05_libero](https://huggingface.co/lerobot/pi05_libero) on LIBERO. Your SO-101 later: finetune.
- **World:** LIBERO $0. SO-101 **$121.94 / $229.88 DIY**.
- **Paper:** `2504.16054_pi05.pdf`, RTC `2506.07339_real_time_chunking.pdf`

The **operations** pick. If you want `lerobot-eval` on LIBERO this week and `lerobot-rollout` on an SO-101 next week, this is the path of least friction. It will not plan like Gemma 4 12B.

## Why it is on the list

- Cross-embodiment pretraining, public LIBERO SOTA-ish numbers, LeRobot-native.
- RTC (NeurIPS 2025) is the right answer to "the world should not pause while the slow net thinks" — sibling Option 6, already implemented (`--inference.type=rtc`).
- Cheap robot is first-class, not an example folder.

## Gaps vs the brief

- PaliGemma 3B reasoning is the e4B failure mode.
- Paper hierarchy (subtasks) is **not** in the open checkpoints. Do not shop for a planner that is not there.
- OpenHelix would not call it dual.
